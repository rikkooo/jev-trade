## 5. Verdict

The released fixture UI **does** present itself as a simulation, **does** keep Jev’s directional distribution, deterministic market risk, and planned position loss in separately labeled cards once a learner reaches a revealed or non-blind workspace, and **does** refuse to treat a zero prospective sample as success.

It does **not** fully meet the v0.1.0 accessibility and public-state contract:

- Dark-theme axe (WCAG 2.2 AA tags) reported **0 violations** on 26 desktop routes, local and deployed.
- Keyboard users can complete the ACME blind-pick journey and see the three measures.
- Material defects remain: skip-link focus, nested `<main>` on not-found, unknown-symbol HTTP 200, first-visit consent covering evidence, 44×44 target failures, light-theme contrast, 200% zoom overflow at 400 CSS pixels, and R12 fields that exist in the view-model but are not shown.

No **BLOCKER** was recorded that would make the fixture appear to execute real trades or advertise a scored edge. Several **MAJOR** items would block a claim that the primary stock view already “reaches WCAG 2.2 AA” or that every required public state is honest under first-visit and zoom conditions.

The 4-of-5 cold-participant comprehension gate is **NOT VERIFIABLE**. An expert heuristic is given separately in §8.

## 8. Comprehension

### 8.1 Protocol (4-of-5 gate)

The v0.1 success criterion and U2/U7 verification require at least four of five **cold** moderated participants, without coaching, to:

1. identify Jev’s directional distribution;
2. identify the deterministic market-risk estimate/index;
3. identify planned position loss;
4. say whether each comes from Jev or from deterministic code;
5. distinguish simulated from real execution;
6. distinguish inconclusive evidence from success.

**Participants:** none were available in this CTT-COMM seat. No five humans were fabricated.

**Gate result: NOT VERIFIABLE.** This is not recorded as a passed 4-of-5 result and is not recorded as a counted 0-of-5 failure of human subjects. U3 / founder validation (`#13`) still owns the human run.

Suggested protocol for that later run (do not treat this as completed):

1. Recruit five adults who have not used Jev Trade and are not authors of v0.1.0.
2. Device: one desktop 1440 and one 400px session per person, or split 3/2.
3. Task: “Use the site until you can explain one current stock decision.” Start at `/`. Do not hint at ACME vs NOVA.
4. After at most 10 minutes, ask the six questions above against the **NOVA** open Position and the **scorecard**, then against **ACME** if they revealed it.
5. Score a participant as a pass only if all six are correct in their own words. Store an anonymized sheet.

### 8.2 Expert heuristic (separate from the gate)

**Conditional pass, with residual risk.** A reviewer who can read labels can separate the three measures on NOVA or on revealed ACME. A truly cold user who stays on the first home viewport, never dismisses analytics, and only opens ACME without scrolling to the triptych can miss them.

| Question | Heuristic | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Jev distribution | Likely identified **if** the learner opens NOVA or reveals ACME | Bars labeled Up/Flat/Down with percents; heading “UP leads the distribution”; eyebrow `FROZEN JEV JUDGMENT` / `JEV JUDGMENT`. |
| Owner = Jev | Likely | “Typed model distribution”; “Jev judges… Deterministic code applies the rules.” |
| Market-risk index | Likely on NOVA | `DETERMINISTIC MARKET RISK` + `32 / 100 · LOW` (NOVA) or `41 / 100 · MEDIUM` (ACME). Copy says “Code combines…”. |
