# Jev Trade vision

## The product

Jev Trade is a transparent market-judgment laboratory that grows into a trading tutor and, only if the evidence earns it, a trusted prediction service.

Markets make confidence seductive and accountability difficult. Jev Trade preserves the moment before the outcome: what information existed, what standard tools showed, what Jev judged, what deterministic policy allowed, what paper action followed, and what happened next. A learner can inspect the whole chain. A researcher can reproduce it. A future customer can judge the record rather than trust a promise.

> Credibility evidence is much more marketable than a spectacular but fragile accuracy claim.

That sentence is the product's compass. Jev Trade succeeds by telling the truth about uncertainty, including the times it abstains, misses, fails, or performs worse than a simple baseline.

## The long arc

Jev Trade advances through evidence-gated stages:

1. **Market game:** people practice decisions with real market structure and fictional capital.
2. **Trading tutor:** each scenario explains the standard signals, Jev's judgment, deterministic risk controls, and realized result.
3. **Evidence lab:** controlled experiments measure the incremental value of Jev across assets, horizons, regimes, and versions.
4. **Predictor service:** authenticated consumers request reproducible predictions and evidence reports through an API.
5. **Paper automation:** users may let the published policy manage simulated positions while retaining full visibility and control.
6. **Real-market automation:** considered only as a separate product and regulatory decision after prospective evidence, operational reliability, data rights, and safety controls justify it.

Progression is not automatic. Each stage may receive a **go**, **adjust**, or **stop** decision. Strong results in one pack do not license claims in another.

## What users should learn

A learner should leave Jev Trade better able to answer five questions:

- What evidence was available when the decision was made?
- What do familiar market tools say, and where do they disagree?
- What did Jev add beyond those tools?
- How much uncertainty and downside did the policy accept?
- Did the decision remain useful after realistic costs and the actual outcome?

The product teaches process before prediction. It makes patience, abstention, sizing discipline, and honest review as visible as being right.

## The four horizons

Jev Trade uses one evidence grammar across four distinct games:

- **Scalping:** seconds to minutes, where spread, depth, latency, slippage, missed fills, and adverse selection can dominate the signal.
- **Day Trading:** minutes to hours, where intraday momentum, VWAP, volume, opening ranges, and scheduled events shape the decision.
- **Swing Trading:** days to weeks, where price structure, overnight gaps, company events, industry context, and patience meet.
- **Long-Term Investing:** months to years, where point-in-time fundamentals, valuation, quality, dividends, and disciplined accumulation matter more than chart noise.

The packs share provenance, judgment, policy, outcome, and scoring contracts. Their data, actions, costs, baselines, and maturity rules remain specific to the horizon.

Scalping, Day, and Swing may simulate long and short positions on fictional capital. Short simulations must model availability, borrow cost, execution failure, and closing behavior; they never imply that the v0.1.0 long-only Position mode placed a short sale.

## The trust contract

Jev Trade will preserve these principles through every version:

- **The future stays hidden.** Predictions use only information available at their cutoff.
- **Published history stays intact.** Corrections and reassessments append evidence; they do not rewrite it.
- **Jev stays bounded.** Jev interprets structured evidence. Code owns arithmetic, market calendars, risk limits, execution, and scoring.
- **Comparisons stay fair.** Standard tools, Jev, the complete policy, and naive controls see equivalent eligible information.
- **Evidence stays complete.** Sample size, coverage, calibration, costs, drawdown, uncertainty, failures, and baselines travel with accuracy.
- **Prospective time is independently provable.** Authoritative forward evidence carries an external timestamp receipt created before the outcome window can resolve.
- **Incremental value is isolated.** Standard-tools and Full Jev Trade policy comparisons hold the deterministic risk, execution, and cost stack constant; product-bundle comparisons are labeled separately.
- **Cost sensitivity is visible.** Base and adverse execution assumptions travel with policy results, and a fragile edge cannot be promoted as robust.
- **Claims stay proportional.** Historical replay informs development. Prospectively frozen outcomes support credibility.
- **Users stay in control.** Automation begins on paper, exposes its state and history, and can be stopped.
- **Rights stay explicit.** A data source enters live or public use only when its license permits the intended fields, retention, model processing, and display.

## Product boundary

Jev Trade does not become a black-box signal seller. It does not hide losing cohorts, present model confidence as probability of profit, or let persuasive language replace risk accounting.

The current program remains simulation-only. Brokerage, deposits, custody, personalized allocation, and real-money execution require a future decision with its own legal, safety, and evidence contract.

## Durable measure of success

The durable outcome is not a single winning percentage. Jev Trade succeeds when people can inspect a decision made before an uncertain future, understand why it was made, measure how it performed against credible alternatives, and trust that an unfavorable result will remain in the record.
